# Merlin's Knight School™ — Titles, Blurbs and Links

*Prepared 31 Oct 2026 for the author website. Every Book 1 claim below was checked against the Book 1 manuscript.*

## Series description

No elite cohort picked them, so six misfit squires end up in the Leftover Yard at Merlin's Knight School, where the great wizard teaches them to think before they charge. Each book is a fair-play mystery for ages 8–14: every clue is on the page, and the squires learn to separate what they can actually see from what they only guess.

- **Short tagline (site):** Six leftover squires, one reluctant wizard, and mysteries solved by clear thinking.
- **Correction for the site's reading-order note:** the site says "Each book is told by a different squire." That isn't so: Books 1–10 are all told by Kay (third person, close on him). Suggested wording: *"Start with Book 1, Trial by Wyvern. Each book solves its own mystery, told through Kay's eyes, and the friendships and running jokes grow from book to book."*

## Book 1 — Trial by Wyvern

**Tagline:** Six leftover squires. One very grumpy wyvern.

**Back-cover blurb (fact-checked and corrected):**

> On their first day at Merlin's Knight School, six misfit squires that no elite cohort picked get exactly three words of instruction from Merlin: "Forest. Problem. Fix it." The problem turns out to be a wyvern sitting on the old rope bridge at Wickerbridge. Merlin's written orders, which said in capital letters DO NOT ENGAGE, are underneath his duck.
>
> Kay, Lance, Gawain, Percival, Gwen and Morgan, together with a boar, a fox, a hawk, a rabbit, a hound and a raven, soon notice that the wyvern is not behaving like a monster at all.
>
> Why would a beast that has hurt no one guard one crumbling tower — and who gains from keeping the bridge closed?

*What changed and why:* the version on the printed back cover and in the print-edition files says the squires were "sent to scout a wyvern … and told, in capital letters, not to engage. Naturally, they engage." In the book, Merlin only says "Forest. Problem. Fix it." (ch. 2). His written order to SCOUT and DO NOT ENGAGE was sat on by Admiral, and the squires read it only after they had already fought the wyvern (ch. 10). Everything else in the blurb checks out:
- the first day (ch. 1);
- the "very old" rope-and-plank bridge and the "crumbling stone tower" (ch. 3);
- "hurt nobody" in nine days (ch. 10);
- the bridge closed "until further notice" (ch. 13).

The printed paperback/hardcover back covers still carry the old wording; they can be rebuilt with this text if you want them to match.

**In this book you'll learn:**
- Merlin's Three Questions: SEE, MEAN, ELSE
- Telling what you see from what you guess
- Why official-looking isn't the same as official
- Noticing what's missing

**Website short blurb (already on the site, accurate):** Trouble has come to the Harrow Gorge, and everyone is ready to blame the wyvern. Kay and the other leftover squires have to look past the obvious answer and follow the clues to the truth.
